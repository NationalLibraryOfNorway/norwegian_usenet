# Step 09: topic modelling

- [01_topic_modelling.py](01_topic_modelling.py) finds topics with turftopic in the one newsgroup `--newsgroup` names, reading its messages from both archives and its embeddings from step 08. Writes to `data/output/09_topic_modelling/<model>/<method>/<newsgroup>[/nr<N>]/`. Run with `--overwrite` to refit a run that is already there; without the flag the script exits without doing anything when the directory holds a `topic_source_counts.jsonl`.
  - `--method` picks the model: `senstopic` (the default), `s3`, `gmm`, `topeax`, `keynmf` or `clustering`, which is the dimensionality reduction and HDBSCAN pipeline of BERTopic and Top2Vec. `topeax` and `clustering` fit on two t-SNE dimensions, with a perplexity of 50 and 15; the other four fit on the embeddings as they are. Every method except `gmm` and `clustering` loads `--model` to embed the vocabulary.
  - `--nr-topics` is picked by `senstopic`, `gmm` and `topeax` when it is left out, needed by `s3` and `keynmf`, and taken by `clustering` as the number of clusters to reduce to after fitting.
  - Written per run: the fitted model, the topic table (`topic_info.csv`), the highest scoring topic of every document in the order they are loaded (`document_topics.npy`), one JSON object per assigned topic with `topic_id`, the `words` of its highest ranking terms, `nb_message_count`, `ia_message_count` and `total_message_count` (`topic_source_counts.jsonl`), and for `topeax` and `clustering` the two dimensions they reduced to (`reduced_embeddings.npy`).
- [02_visualize_topics.py](02_visualize_topics.py) plots the messages of the one newsgroup that `--newsgroup` names in two dimensions, coloured by topic and shaped by archive, reading the `document_topics.npy` and `topic_info.csv` of the run that `--method`, `--newsgroup` and `--nr-topics` name. A `--method topeax` or `--method clustering` run is plotted over the two t-SNE dimensions it found its topics in, read from its `reduced_embeddings.npy`, and the other methods over the step 08 UMAP embeddings, which need to be computed for that newsgroup alone, i.e. step 08 run with `--selection <newsgroup>`. Opens in a browser, and with `--save-fig` also writes `topics.png` to the run directory.
